Back for our 30th edition, Electric Spring continues as a pioneering force in experimental music with some added audiovisual delights. We've got an international cast of performers this year, ready to entertain you with the fruits of their imaginations - read through the rest of this Programme to find out about all of the artists and the work they're presenting to you.

Come along to the <i>Richard Steinitz building</i> at the University of Huddersfield from 17:00 on the 18th-21st February 2026. Spend some time in the installations, then at 19:00 make your way up to the Phipps Hall on the 2nd floor for the concerts through our 64-loudspeaker immersive sound system. All of the events and installations on the University campus are free of charge.

They'll end around 21:00 and then you can make your way into town to Hive Cafe, for the late-night sessions from our partner, Float, for which there is a modest charge.

<b>A note on accessibility</b>: <br> 
<i>Phipps Hall</i> is on the 2nd floor. Access is by stairs or either of 2 lifts and is step-free. There are gendered and accessible toilets on the Ground and Second floors and Evac-chairs in case of emergency.

<i>Hive Cafe</i> has a step to access, but a ramp is available. There is a gender neutral and accessible toilet. We will give free entry to any required companion for an audience member with a disability or additional needs - just explain on arrival or email in advance to <a href="mailto:float@gmail.com" class="email">float@gmail.com</a> so we're expecting you, if you'd prefer. We will not ask to see any proof of disability or carer status.

Electric Spring Festival is a University of Huddersfield event, organised in conjunction with the Centre for Research in New Music.
